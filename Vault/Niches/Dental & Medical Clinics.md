# Niche: Dental & Medical Clinics

**Priority: Tier 1 — Chase first**

---

## Why This Niche

- Huge fragmented market. Most clinics have zero or terrible websites
- Patients Google them daily → bad website = lost patients
- Decision maker = doctor/owner → short sales cycle (days not weeks)
- Pain point is visceral: "We lose patients to competitors who look more professional online"
- Chatbot ROI is obvious and immediate

---

## What They Buy

| Product | Price |
|---------|-------|
| Starter website + basic chatbot | ₹35,000 + ₹8k/mo |

---

## Chatbot Use Cases

1. **Appointment booking** — "I'd like to see Dr. Sharma on Tuesday at 3pm"
2. **FAQ answering** — Timings, services, insurance, parking, doctors available
3. **Lead capture** — Name + phone before any info given
4. **Show-up reminders** — WhatsApp at 23hrs before appointment
5. **Post-visit review request** — 48hrs after appointment

---

## Key Pain Points to Probe

- "How many patients call after hours and you miss them?"
- "What % of bookings actually show up?"
- "How much time does your receptionist spend answering the same 5 questions?"
- "What happens when someone books online and doesn't confirm?"

## Single Automation Use Case (Autera Tier 1 — ₹9,999/mo)

**"Appointment Recovery System"**

Flow:
```
Patient books via chatbot
    → WhatsApp reminder sent 23 hours before
    → Patient confirms: ✅ appointment held
    → Patient ignores: 📲 follow-up at T-2hrs
    → Patient cancels: 🔄 rebooking flow triggered
    → Post-visit (48hrs): review request sent
```

ROI pitch:
> "Most clinics lose 15–20% of bookings to no-shows. If you book 100 patients/month at ₹500 average, that's ₹10,000 lost monthly. This system reduces no-shows to 5–8%. You recover ₹7,000–8,500/month. System costs ₹9,999. Pays for itself + profit in month 1."
