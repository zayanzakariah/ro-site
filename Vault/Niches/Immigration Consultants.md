# Niche: Immigration & Visa Consultants

**Priority: Tier 1 — Chase first (alongside dental)**

---

## Why This Niche

- Booming sector: Canada, Australia, Germany, UK demand is massive in India
- Clients are high-intent (immigrating = life decision) → high willingness to pay
- Consultants are cash-rich, often flooded with low-quality leads
- Decision maker = firm owner → fast decisions
- 24/7 qualification is an obvious win (inquiries come at all hours)

## Chatbot Use Cases

1. **Country eligibility checker** — "Tell me about your education, age, work experience"
2. **Document checklist** — Country-specific list of required documents
3. **Appointment booking** — "Book a free consultation with our advisor"
4. **Lead qualification** — Filter hot (eligible, motivated) vs cold (not eligible, just browsing)
5. **FAQ** — Processing times, fees, success rates, office location

## Operations System Use Case (Autera Tier 2 — ₹25,000/mo)

**"Lead Qualification + Pipeline CRM"**

Flow:
```
Lead submits eligibility form
    → Claude API analyzes: High/Medium/Low fit
    → High fit: "You qualify for Canada Express Entry" + calendar link
    → Medium fit: 3-email nurture sequence over 7 days + call prompt
    → Low fit: "Not eligible now — here's what you need to improve" + re-engage in 6 months
    → All leads stored in Supabase CRM
    → Consultant dashboard: 45 leads, 12 qualified, 3 hot (sorted by conversion probability)
```

ROI pitch:
> "Right now you're manually filtering 80 leads to find 8 good ones — that's 3–4 hours/week. Autera does that automatically. You close 15–18 leads/month instead of 8–10. At ₹2–5L per engagement, that's ₹10–20L incremental revenue. System costs ₹25k/mo. Math is obvious."
