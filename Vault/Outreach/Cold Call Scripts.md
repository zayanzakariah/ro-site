# Cold Call Scripts — India

*Rule: Always lead with the demo, not the price. Get to WhatsApp ASAP.*

---

## Universal Opener

**English:**
> "Hi, am I speaking with [Name]? I'm [Your Name] calling from RO. We work with [clinics/consultants/real estate firms] to help them get more clients through their website and AI. I actually looked at your business and had a quick idea I wanted to share — do you have 2 minutes?"

**Hindi-English mix (works better in North India, Tier 2 cities):**
> "Hi, [Name] ji? Main [Your Name] bol raha hoon RO se. Hum [dental clinics/immigration firms] ke liye websites aur AI assistants banate hain. Aapki business dekhi — ek quick idea share karna tha. Kya 2 minute hain?"

---

## If They Say Yes

> "We've built an AI assistant specifically for [dental clinics / immigration firms] that answers patient questions, books appointments, and qualifies leads — even at 2am. I'd love to show you a 5-minute demo — no cost, no obligation. Can I send you a WhatsApp link right now?"

---

## Objection Handling

**"We already have a website"**
> "That's great! This isn't about replacing your website — it's about adding an AI layer that handles the follow-up your team currently does manually. Most clinics we work with are losing 10–15 patients/month just because no one follows up fast enough. Can I show you a quick demo of how it works?"

**"We're not interested in AI"**
> "Totally understand — a lot of [doctors/consultants] I talk to feel the same way initially. What usually changes their mind is seeing how it works in practice. It's not a robot — it just handles the basic questions so your staff can focus on actual patients. 5-minute demo, no obligation. Can I send you a link?"

**"We don't have budget right now"**
> "Makes sense — that's actually why we do demos first. Most clients find the system pays for itself in 4–6 weeks (it books more appointments than it costs). If after the demo it doesn't make sense, no problem. Can I send the link on WhatsApp?"

**"Send me an email"**
> "For sure! I'll send it. But can I also send you the demo link on WhatsApp? It's a 5-minute experience — most clients prefer seeing it live before the email. What's the best number?"

---

## Getting to WhatsApp

> "Great! I'll send you the demo link on WhatsApp right now — it's a live chatbot built for [their niche], you can actually talk to it. What's the best number to reach you on?"

**[After they give number]:**
> "Perfect. Sending now. It'll come from [your WhatsApp number]. Take a look when you get a chance — I'll follow up in a day or two. Is afternoon better for you, or evening?"

---

## Call Closing (Book Discovery Call)

> "Based on what you've shared, this would definitely work for [their business]. Can we block 20 minutes this week to walk through it in more detail — I can show you specifically how it'd work for [their clinic/firm]? I have [Tuesday 3pm / Wednesday 11am] — which works?"

---

## Key Rules

1. Always lead with the demo, not the price
2. Get to WhatsApp as fast as possible — voice notes + demo link convert better than email in India
3. Follow up 3 times minimum (most deals close on follow-up 2 or 3)
4. Mirror their language — if they speak Hindi, respond in Hindi
5. Reference their specific business — shows you've done your homework
