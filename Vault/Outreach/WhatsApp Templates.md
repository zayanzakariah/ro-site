One clinic went 60% → 85% show-up in 4 weeks. That's ₹1.5L+ back monthly. Interested in a 15-min call? 🎯

**Template 2 (Problem-first, Hindi mix):**
> Dr. [Name], ek second! 😊 Aapka website dekha — but notice kiya? Jab patient book karte hain, half nahi show up hote. ₹50k missed revenue per month. Hum 1 system banaye — automatic reminder + follow-up. Clinic ne bataya: "Mera cancellation 50% se 15% ho gaya." ₹1L+ bachaya monthly. Worth 15 min to see? https://[demo-link]

**Template 3 (Competitor angle, urgency):**
> Hi Dr. [Name] — [Nearby Clinic] just automated their appointment reminders + follow-ups. They're seeing 85% show-up rates now (vs 60% before). Free 15-min demo? Happy to show how it works 👇
