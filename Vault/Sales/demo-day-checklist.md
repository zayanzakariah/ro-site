# Demo Day Checklist — Walk-In Pitch Prep

**Before you leave for the office: 90-min prep**

---

## RESEARCH (15 min)

- [ ] Google Maps: Check reviews (look for "rude staff", "late response", "can't reach them")
- [ ] Website: Outdated? Many spelling errors? Good ammunition.
- [ ] Social media: Are they posting regularly? Do posts get engagement?
- [ ] JustDial / Google My Business: Check service areas, staff count, hours

**Write down:** 1–2 specific pain points you spotted.
Example: "3 reviews saying 'couldn't reach them for days'" → Lead follow-up bot

---

## BOT CUSTOMIZATION (30 min)

**Create a demo bot instance with their data:**

- [ ] **Business name:** [Insert their clinic/agency name]
- [ ] **Welcome message:** Personalized to their niche
  - Clinic: "Hi! Welcome to [Dr. Name's] Clinic. Can I help you book an appointment?"
  - Agent: "Hi! Looking for a property in [City]? Let's find your perfect home."
  - Educator: "Hi! Interested in our [course name]? Tell me more about your goals."
- [ ] **Bot personality:** Match their tone (formal for law firm, warm for clinic)
- [ ] **Sample lead flow:** Appointment booking / inquiry / demo class request → confirmation message
- [ ] **Mock confirmations:** Show appointment scheduled email, WhatsApp reminder in 2 days

**Test locally:**
- Ask for appointment as "Rajesh Kumar"
- Check bot response
- Show confirmation message they'd receive

---

## DEMO FLOW (Verbatim Walk-Through)

**Phone / Tablet ready, bot link bookmarked. DO THIS ON THEIR DEVICE:**

```
"Let me show you what this looks like for your business.

[Hand them phone]

Go ahead—ask for [appointment/demo/info]. Just like a customer would.
Ask it something tricky, or test it out. I'll show you what happens next."

[Customer types inquiry]

"See? Instant response. No waiting for your team.

Now here's where it gets good—[30 seconds later]
they get a WhatsApp reminder. On their phone. Even if they forgot."

[Show mock reminder screenshot]

"And all of this happens automatically. Your team is free to close deals,
not chase leads. One clinic used this and recovered 5 missed appointments
in month 1 alone. ₹20K extra that month.

Any questions so far?"
```

---

## PITCH MATERIALS (Printed / Digital)

- [ ] **1-pager:** Pricing, benefits, next steps (laminated for reuse)
- [ ] **Case study:** 1 similar niche (e.g., if pitching clinic: one clinic's results)
- [ ] **Phone:** Bot demo live and tested
- [ ] **Tablet/laptop:** Ready to screenshare if needed
- [ ] **Business cards:** Name, WhatsApp, Calendly link on back
- [ ] **Proposal template:** Pre-filled with their business name, pricing as per tier

---

## SCREENSHARE BACKUP (If WiFi Fails)

- [ ] Pre-recorded 2-min demo video (phone screen recording)
- [ ] PDF with bot flow diagram
- [ ] Screenshots of similar client's results (anonymized)

---

## ROOM SETUP (Before They Arrive)

- [ ] Quiet space (not reception where phones ring)
- [ ] Chair across from them (not at desk—too formal)
- [ ] Water bottle / coffee ready (small rapport builder)
- [ ] Phone on silent (zero interruptions)
- [ ] Laptop closed until demo time

---

## CONVERSATION ROADMAP

**Minute 1-2:** Rapport
```
"Thanks for making time. How's business been? [Listen.]
Yeah, growth is good but painful in its own way, right?"
```

**Minute 3-5:** Problem Statement
```
"Here's what I'm curious about—when a patient/lead/inquiry comes in,
walk me through what happens. [Listen for pain.]"
```

**Minute 6-10:** Demo + Quantify
```
"Okay, so you're losing maybe ₹X per month. Let me show you how we solve this..."
[Hand phone. Live demo. Show them the bot in action.]
```

**Minute 11-15:** Close + Next Step
```
"So here's what I'd recommend: 15,000 setup, 9,999/month.
Pays for itself in under a month. We go live in 5 days.
Questions?"
[Silence. Wait for them.]

If hot: "Let's draft a proposal right now. I'll email it tonight, you sign, we start."
If warm: "Think it over. But let's lock in a follow-up call for [day]."
```

---

## RED FLAGS TO ABORT / ADJUST

| Flag | Action |
|---|---|
| "We don't use WhatsApp / don't have WiFi" | Pivot: Show website chatbot instead |
| "Our staff won't accept automation" | Reframe: "Staff loves it. It does the boring stuff, they close deals." |
| "Send it to [person who isn't here]" | "Great. Want them on a quick call now, or should I schedule for 3 of us?" |
| "We already tried something like this" | "What didn't work?" [Listen. Explain difference. Show why this works.] |
| Constant interruptions / disrespect | Keep it short. "Let me not take more of your time. Here's the proposal." |

---

## AFTER YOU LEAVE

**Same day (within 4 hours):**
- [ ] Send proposal via WhatsApp
- [ ] Follow-up message: "Added one more thing—here's a case study of [similar biz]. Let me know thoughts."

**If warm (48h later):**
- [ ] Re-engage: "Anything jumped out? Any questions I can clarify?"

**If hot:**
- [ ] Call next morning: "Ready to start?" [Close for payment.]

---

## BATTLE SCARS (Common Goofs)

❌ Demoing on YOUR phone (they can't see clearly)
❌ Talking too much instead of showing
❌ Forgetting to customize bot for their business (looks generic)
❌ Leaving without a next-step date locked in
❌ Sending proposal 2 days later (momentum dies)

✓ Demo on THEIR phone or tablet
✓ Show in 5 mins, let them play with it, then explain
✓ Bot says their name, their business, their pain
✓ Before you leave: "Great. Let's lock in Thursday at 4 PM for follow-up."
✓ Proposal sent same day via WhatsApp, personal note
