# Discovery Call Script (20 min)

**Goal:** Understand their pain → quantify impact → qualify → demo offer → next step

---

## OPENING (2 min)

```
"Hi [Name]! Thanks for taking the call. I'm Zayan, we help
[niche: clinics / real estate / education] automate the repetitive stuff—
appointment reminders, lead follow-ups, review collection.

Quick housekeeping: I'll ask you a few questions, then show you
how we've solved this for similar businesses. Does that work?"
```

**Pause for "yes"**

---

## RAPPORT (1 min)

**Pick one:**
- Clinic: "How long have you been running [clinic name]? What's been the biggest challenge this year?"
- Real Estate: "How many projects are you juggling right now?"
- Education: "What's your current student intake looking like?"
- Immigration: "How many cases are you handling per month?"

**Listen. One genuine follow-up.** ("Wow, that's rough..." / "That's impressive growth.")

---

## PAIN DISCOVERY (5 min)

**Ask in order. Wait for full answer.**

**Q1:** "Walk me through a typical lead. From the moment someone calls or messages you, what happens?"
> *Listen for:* Manual steps, delays, dropped leads, staff friction.

**Q2:** "How many leads or appointments slip through each month because of follow-up delays?"
> *Listen for:* Number. ("Maybe 3–4...") Pause. Don't prompt.

**Q3:** "And if you could recover those, what's the average value per lead/appointment?"
> *Listen for:* Revenue impact. Clinic: ₹3K–5K per appointment. Agent: ₹25K–50K per deal.

**Q4:** "Is your team spending time on repetitive stuff they'd rather not do? What takes up most of their day?"
> *Listen for:* Appointment reminders, WhatsApp follow-ups, booking calls, email chasing.

---

## QUANTIFY (3 min)

**Do math aloud with them:**

**Clinic example:**
```
"So you said maybe 4–5 missed appointments a month, and each one's
worth roughly ₹4,000. That's about ₹20,000 in lost revenue per month.
Over a year, that's ₹2,40,000 you're leaving on the table."
[Pause.]
"And if we sent reminders automatically—WhatsApp 2 days before—
you could probably recover at least 60% of those. That's ₹12,000 a month."
```

**Real Estate example:**
```
"If you're closing 1–2 extra deals per quarter because leads aren't
falling through, that's ₹100K–200K in commissions. Is that fair?"
```

**Education example:**
```
"You said 10–15 inquiry leads a month. If 30% are dropping because
follow-up's slow, that's 3–4 lost enrollments a month at ₹10K–20K each.
₹30K–80K a month in lost revenue."
```

---

## PIVOT TO SOLUTION (2 min)

```
"Here's what we've done for [similar business]: automated WhatsApp
reminders, lead capture, and follow-ups. No human involved until
they're actually ready to book or buy.

One clinic went from 40% no-shows to 15% in 60 days.
One realtor recovered 2–3 deals/quarter that were ghosting.

The cost? ₹15,000 setup + ₹9,999/month for a single automation.
Pays for itself in under a month."
```

**Pause. Read the room.**

---

## DEMO OFFER (3 min)

**Option A (Hot prospect):**
```
"I want to show you exactly how this works. Can I screen-share
a demo real quick? It'll take 3 minutes."
[Share screen. Show bot in their niche, with their pain point.]
```

**Option B (Warmer vibe needed):**
```
"I think this could save you ₹12K–20K a month. Can we grab 15 minutes
next Tuesday for a quick demo? Just you and me, live example with your
type of business."
```

---

## NEXT STEP (1 min)

**If hot:**
```
"So here's what I'd do: send you a one-page proposal with exact pricing
and timeline. We'd onboard in 5 days. Sound good?"
[If yes: "Perfect. I'll WhatsApp it over in 30 mins."]
```

**If warm:**
```
"Great. Let's schedule a 15-min demo for [Tuesday/Thursday] at 10 AM.
I'll send a calendar link. Deal?"
[Confirm time. Send link immediately after call.]
```

**If cold:**
```
"I get it. Tell you what—I'll send a quick one-pager showing what
this looks like for your business. No pressure. If it clicks,
let's grab a demo. Fair?"
[Send proposal 1 hour later via WhatsApp.]
```

---

## CLOSES

**Objection: "This sounds good, but I need to talk to [partner/wife/accountant]"**
```
"Totally. Want them on the next call? Or I can send a proposal
and you loop them in?"
```

**Objection: "Send me a proposal and we'll discuss"**
```
"Sure. Here's what I'll do—send the proposal + a 2-minute video demo
(your industry, same problem). Give it a look, and let's hop on
a quick 10-min call to Q&A. That way you've seen it and have questions ready. Deal?"
```

**Objection: "Sounds expensive"**
```
"I hear you. But do the math with me: ₹10K a month vs ₹20K you're
losing now in dropped leads. What's that trade-off worth to you?"
```
