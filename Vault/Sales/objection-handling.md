# Objection Handling — Top 12 Indian SMB Rebuttals

**Format:** Objection → 2-3 line response (verbatim, use exactly as written)

---

## 1. "Too Expensive"

**Objection:** "₹10K/month is too much for us right now."

**Response:**
```
I hear it. But walk me through the math: How many leads/appointments
are you losing monthly? Because if it's 4–5, that's ₹15K–20K right there.
What's paying ₹10K to recover ₹15K+ worth to you?
```

---

## 2. "Already Have a Website / Bot"

**Objection:** "We have a website. Don't need anything else."

**Response:**
```
Got it. Is it bringing in leads consistently? Because most websites
sit there collecting dust. Our bots actually respond to customers 24/7—
even at midnight when your team's sleeping. Different animal.
```

---

## 3. "WhatsApp is Enough"

**Objection:** "We just use WhatsApp manually. That's working fine."

**Response:**
```
It's working, but how many hours a week is your team on WhatsApp
doing the same follow-up over and over? If it's 5+ hours, you're
losing ₹5K–10K in staff cost alone. Automation lets them focus on closing.
```

---

## 4. "Send a Proposal, We'll Discuss"

**Objection:** "Interesting. Send me a proposal and we'll talk internally."

**Response:**
```
Sure, I'll send it. But here's what I'll also do—send a 2-minute
video demo showing exactly how this works for your business. Give it a look,
and let's hop on a 10-min call so you've got questions ready. Deal?
```

---

## 5. "Need to Talk to My Partner"

**Objection:** "This sounds good, but I need to check with my partner/wife/accountant."

**Response:**
```
Totally makes sense. Want me to jump on a call with both of you?
Or I can send the proposal and you loop them in, then we all connect
if they have questions. Your call.
```

---

## 6. "We Don't Have Time to Set This Up"

**Objection:** "Sounds good, but we're slammed right now. No bandwidth."

**Response:**
```
That's exactly why you need this—you're too busy to handle follow-ups manually.
We handle setup completely (5 days, done). Your team just uses it.
Zero extra work from you guys on day 1.
```

---

## 7. "Our Business is Different"

**Objection:** "Our business is unique. This won't work for us."

**Response:**
```
I get it. Most people say that. But we've done this for [niche] clinics/agents/institutes
in 10+ cities. The automation is different for each, but the logic is the same—
capture leads faster, follow up automatically, close more.
Can I show you an example from someone like you?
```

---

## 8. "Don't Trust AI / Worried It'll Break"

**Objection:** "AI sometimes says weird stuff. Won't it mess up our reputation?"

**Response:**
```
Legit concern. That's why we train the bot on your exact scripts and FAQs.
It's not ChatGPT doing random stuff—it's your responses, word-for-word.
Plus, complex questions go straight to your team. AI handles the simple stuff only.
```

---

## 9. "This is a One-Time Project, Not Monthly"

**Objection:** "We don't need monthly services. Just a website redesign, then we're done."

**Response:**
```
Right, but here's the thing—once it's live, you need someone monitoring it,
updating bots when promotions change, fixing errors, tweaking copy.
That's ongoing. Most clients do 1-2 tweaks a month, minimum.
Or you can babysit it yourself. Your call.
```

---

## 10. "I'll Think About It"

**Objection:** "Sounds interesting. Let me think about it and get back to you."

**Response:**
```
Sure. But thinking without seeing the actual bot is tough. Let me ask—
what would make a difference? Is it the price, the timeline, or do you
want to see it working first? Let's solve that today.
```

---

## 11. "We Have Limited Data / Clean Database"

**Objection:** "Our WhatsApp list is messy. Lots of wrong numbers."

**Response:**
```
That's okay. We clean it in day 1—pull real phone numbers from your
CRM or Google reviews. And each message you send trains the bot to
know who actually engages. It gets better every month.
```

---

## 12. "What If I'm Not Happy?"

**Objection:** "What if we start this and it doesn't work?"

**Response:**
```
Fair question. We give you 30 days to test it. If it's not working,
you can exit with 30 days' notice and keep your data. But I'm confident—
we only do this if we know it'll save you money. That's the bet.
```

---

## UNIVERSAL DEFENSE PATTERNS

**When they deflect:**
```
I get it, you're busy. But this will buy you time, not cost it.
15 mins to see the demo. If it doesn't click, no harm.
```

**When they say "call me next week":**
```
Sure. But let's lock it in now so it actually happens.
[Send calendar link immediately.]
```

**When they compare to competitors:**
```
What problem did [other solution] solve? Because if it worked,
we wouldn't be having this call. Let me show you what we do differently.
```

**When they ghost after proposal:**
```
[After 5 days, no response]
Hey [Name], checking in. Did you get a chance to look? Any quick questions?
[If still nothing after 7 days: Archive and move on.]
```

---

## Golden Rule
**Never argue. Always ask.** If they say "too expensive," don't defend price.
Ask: "What would you need to see for this to make sense?"
Then listen.
